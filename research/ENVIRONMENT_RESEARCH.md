## Verified API choice

Verifiers main pinned to `3bbac389691a1ee31f6b3ae431921b5c525f3fed`.
Prime-envs read at `c4d04dfe212c153a587ea4ce072ae6753e74d6e9`.
Current native V1 plugin exports a Taskset subclass through `__all__`, not a legacy
`StatefulToolEnv`. `vf.load_environment(EnvConfig)` composes the taskset and harness.
Our convenience `load_environment` delegates to that real loader. The built-in `null`
harness is a tool-capable chat loop (despite its name); no shell tool is needed.

Decision: implement ten local deterministic tools, one `vf.Toolset`, typed rollout state,
one Task/Taskset, `@vf.reward`, independent `@vf.metric` business success, `@vf.stop`.
No service images, second model, downloaded dataset or real customer data required.
